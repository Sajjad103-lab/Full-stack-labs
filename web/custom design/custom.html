💡 Flexbox aligns multiple elements easily in rows or columns without float hacks.
💡 Box Model consists of content space, padding inside, border, and outer margin.
💡 Gradients create smooth multi-color transitions across elements.
💡 Box Shadows add realistic depth or glowing aura effects to boxes.
